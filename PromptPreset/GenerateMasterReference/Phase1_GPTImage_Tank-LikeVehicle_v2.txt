Create a clean multi-view concept sheet of a stylized, strongly deformed low-poly historical tank.

VEHICLE:
[ VEHICLE NAME ]

The specified vehicle must remain immediately recognizable as that exact tank model or variant.

Preserve its characteristic:

* hull architecture
* turret architecture
* gun and mantlet
* suspension type
* road-wheel arrangement
* drive sprocket and idler arrangement
* track structure
* major hatches
* engine deck
* front and rear armor forms
* major external equipment
* characteristic national and period silhouette

Do not accidentally convert the vehicle into another model, another variant, or a generic tank.

==================================================
PRIMARY STYLIZATION RULE
========================

Apply a deliberate and clearly visible compact deformation.

THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE IS:

STRONGLY COMPRESS THE VEHICLE ALONG ITS FRONT-TO-BACK / LONGITUDINAL AXIS.

The vehicle must look as if the original historical tank has been physically compressed from front to rear.

The deformation should be immediately readable as:

SHORT
+
STUBBY
+
CHUNKY
+
COMPACT
+
RELATIVELY WIDE

It must NOT look like a normally proportioned tank that has simply been scaled smaller.

==================================================
LONGITUDINAL COMPRESSION
========================

Significantly shorten:

* overall hull length
* wheelbase
* longitudinal spacing between suspension units
* distance between front armor, turret, engine deck, and rear armor
* unnecessary empty space along the vehicle length

The shortened hull must be obvious in both the HERO and SIDE views.

Target approximately the visual impression of a vehicle whose hull has been compressed to roughly:

70–80% of its normal apparent front-to-back proportion,

while preserving most of its original apparent width.

This is a visual stylization target, not an engineering dimension.

==================================================
WHAT SHOULD NOT SHRINK EQUALLY
==============================

Do NOT uniformly scale all dimensions.

Keep relatively substantial:

* hull width
* track width
* turret size
* gun caliber impression
* suspension wheel diameter
* sprocket size
* idler size
* major hatches
* characteristic armor structures

The width-to-length ratio should become noticeably larger than on the real vehicle.

The tank should therefore feel:

WIDER FOR ITS LENGTH
and
SHORTER FOR ITS WIDTH.

==================================================
TURRET / GUN PROPORTION
=======================

Keep the turret visually prominent.

The turret may become slightly oversized relative to the shortened hull because the hull is being compressed more strongly than the turret.

Do NOT excessively shrink the turret to match the hull compression.

The gun should preserve its characteristic visual length and caliber impression.

A relatively long gun projecting from a very short hull is acceptable and often desirable.

This contrast reinforces the compact stylization.

==================================================
SUSPENSION / RUNNING GEAR
=========================

Preserve the original suspension TYPE and mechanical identity.

Preserve the characteristic:

* number and grouping of major road wheels
* bogie arrangement
* interleaved / overlapping wheel language where historically applicable
* return roller arrangement where visually important
* sprocket location
* idler location
* track routing

Unless an explicit vehicle-specific instruction says otherwise:

DO NOT REMOVE ROAD WHEELS OR SUSPENSION UNITS JUST TO MAKE THE TANK SHORTER.

Instead:

* reduce spacing between repeated components
* compress the running gear longitudinally
* slightly enlarge important wheels where useful
* simplify mechanical geometry
* exaggerate large readable suspension forms

The tracks should occupy a large portion of the side silhouette.

The running gear should feel chunky and mechanically readable.

==================================================
TARGET SILHOUETTE
=================

The vehicle should read as:

REAL HISTORICAL TANK
→ compressed front-to-back
→ large original identity cues preserved
→ simplified mechanical construction
→ chunky low-poly game asset

The result should be more strongly deformed than a realistic scale model.

However, do not push the design into super-deformed cartoon territory.

Vehicle identity must remain stronger than the caricature.

==================================================
LOW-POLY DESIGN LANGUAGE
========================

Use clean, deliberately simplified low-poly geometry.

Prioritize:

* large primary armor volumes
* clear silhouette
* planar surfaces
* readable turret geometry
* clear hull / turret / gun separation
* large readable wheels
* simplified but understandable tracks
* large recognizable mechanical structures
* controlled bevels

Simplify:

* bolts
* handles
* hinges
* tools
* track links
* grilles
* weld lines
* cables
* brackets
* small fittings

Remove insignificant micro-detail.

Avoid dense realistic geometry.

The design should look practical to reproduce as a real-time 3D game asset.

==================================================
MATERIAL / TEXTURE
==================

Use an extremely simple grayscale presentation.

MATERIAL:

* grayscale diffuse only
* subtle grayscale value separation
* very light ambient occlusion
* non-metallic appearance
* high roughness
* nearly matte
* minimal specular response

NO:

* realistic PBR surface treatment
* metallic reflections
* camouflage
* complex textures
* rust
* mud
* dirt
* scratches
* chipped paint
* heavy weathering
* procedural noise

Most visual information must come from:

GEOMETRY
+
SILHOUETTE
+
GRAYSCALE VALUE
+
SOFT SHADOW
+
SUBTLE AO

Optional simple historical markings may be used sparingly.

==================================================
HERO VIEW
=========

The primary panel should contain a large front 3/4 isometric HERO view.

Camera:

* slightly elevated
* approximately orthographic or long-lens appearance
* minimal perspective distortion
* entire vehicle visible

The compact longitudinal deformation must be clearly visible.

The hero view must emphasize:

* short hull
* wide stance
* large turret
* large running gear
* characteristic gun
* recognizable vehicle identity

Avoid cinematic wide-angle distortion.

==================================================
REFERENCE VIEWS
===============

Include consistent views of exactly the same design:

1. FRONT
2. SIDE
3. REAR
4. TOP
5. FRONT 3/4
6. REAR 3/4

The SIDE view is critically important.

The SIDE view must clearly communicate:

* shortened overall length
* compressed wheelbase
* compressed suspension spacing
* stocky hull proportion
* preserved suspension identity
* turret-to-hull proportion
* gun length relative to compact hull

All panels must depict the same model.

Do not redesign or change proportions between views.

==================================================
MODEL CONSISTENCY
=================

Treat the large HERO model as the authoritative design.

All other panels must explain that same design.

Keep consistent:

* road-wheel count
* suspension grouping
* sprocket location
* idler location
* turret dimensions
* hull dimensions
* track width
* gun length
* gun mantlet
* hatch positions
* engine-deck layout
* external fittings
* panel boundaries
* repeated component placement

Occlusion may hide components.

It must never create or delete components between views.

==================================================
DETAIL REFERENCES
=================

Include useful close-ups selected from:

* suspension / bogie
* road wheels
* drive sprocket
* idler
* track
* gun mantlet
* turret front
* turret side
* commander hatch / cupola
* engine deck
* rear hull
* characteristic armor structure
* distinctive external equipment

Detail panels must show the SAME geometry.

Do not invent finer or contradictory details only in close-up panels.

==================================================
REFERENCE-SHEET PRESENTATION
============================

Present everything as a professional 3D modeling reference / game-asset concept sheet.

Use:

* structured grid layout
* generous spacing
* neutral gray or dark-gray background
* consistent illumination
* consistent scale
* minimal labels
* low visual clutter

No environment.
No characters.
No battlefield.
No explosions.
No cinematic effects.

==================================================
LIGHTING
========

Use simple studio-style illumination.

* large soft key
* weak fill
* subtle contact shadow
* subtle AO
* neutral color temperature
* no strong rim light
* no colored light
* no glossy highlights

Lighting should explain geometry rather than dramatize it.

==================================================
PRIORITY ORDER
==============

1. Exact vehicle identity
2. Strong longitudinal compression
3. Short, stubby, chunky silhouette
4. Large width-to-length ratio
5. Preservation of suspension identity
6. Large readable turret and running gear
7. Clean low-poly geometry
8. Grayscale diffuse + subtle AO
9. Consistent multi-view reference
10. Practical modeling readability

==================================================
FINAL VISUAL TARGET
===================

A recognizable historical tank transformed into a deliberately shortened and chunky low-poly game vehicle.

The vehicle should look as though the REAL TANK itself was compressed along its longitudinal axis.

It must NOT look like a realistic scale model simply rendered smaller.

The defining impression should be:

SAME TANK
+
SAME MECHANICAL IDENTITY
+
MUCH SHORTER HULL
+
MUCH SHORTER WHEELBASE
+
SUBSTANTIAL WIDTH
+
LARGE READABLE RUNNING GEAR
+
PROMINENT TURRET
